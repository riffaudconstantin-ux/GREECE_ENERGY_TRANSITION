# GREECE ENERGY TRANSITION
## OFFICIAL DATA SOURCES

### 1. Electricity Generation
Source: Eurostat
Dataset: nrg_cb_pem
Description: Monthly net electricity generation by fuel type.
URL: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/nrg_cb_pem/default/table

### 2. Annual Electricity Production
Source: Eurostat
Dataset: nrg_bal_peh
Description: Annual electricity production by fuel type.
URL: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/nrg_bal_peh/default/table

### 3. Electricity Prices
Source: Eurostat
Dataset: nrg_pc_205
Description: Electricity prices for non-household consumers.
URL: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/nrg_pc_205/default/table

### 4. Complete Energy Balances
Source: Eurostat
Dataset: nrg_bal_c
Description: Annual national energy balances.
URL: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/nrg_bal_c/default/table
